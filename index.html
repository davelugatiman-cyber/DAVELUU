The Developer

My focus is responsive combat - hit reactions, stagger, i-frames and animation-driven feedback that make every exchange feel intentional. Around that core I build ability systems with clean data-driven design, enemy AI that reads the player's rhythm, and procedural worlds that stay interesting on repeat runs.

I work fast and I learn fast. Prototypes land in days, get playtested, then get tuned. I care about frame budget, clean architecture, and builds that a whole team can extend without a rewrite.
